# Voice profile

Personal to the student who uses this copy of the skill. Delete the `voice/` folder before sharing the skill; a new user gets the setup interview instead.

## Background

- Native language: Ukrainian. Uses English daily at work, but doesn't know many idioms or rare/advanced words. Text full of fancy vocabulary or idioms sounds fake and is hard to defend if a grader or peer asks about it.
- Work context and main source of examples: software developer on a remote, international team across time zones, colleagues with mixed English levels, mostly written and asynchronous communication.

## Style (from `sample.md`, a discussion forum post)

- Ordinary words. Medium-length sentences that are a bit loose, not short and even. Avoid neatly balanced pairs like "A stack is X, so Y. A queue is X, so Y." or "worked well, while ... did not"; real writing is less symmetrical. No contractions, idioms, rhetorical questions, or em dashes.
- Slightly informal constructions are fine ("ignoring our cross-team guidelines", "all because of one message he never sent"). Don't polish them into corporate prose.
- Examples are specific and real: who did what, what happened, what it cost or saved the team.
- In-text citations at the end of the sentence that uses the source; one or two per paragraph.
- Repeats a key word instead of swapping in synonyms.
- First person where the task allows.

## Forum posts

- Opens with "Hello everyone," and states the position in the first sentence ("To me, X means...").
- Flow: define the concept with course citations, explain why it matters, connect it to the work context, give concrete examples (one good and one bad works well when the topic allows), end on one plain sentence that ties them together, as the last sentence of the final paragraph, not a paragraph of its own. The work context should explain why the point matters (for example, why clear writing matters more across time zones), not just appear as a job title.
- No headings, bullets, or bold. Three to four solid paragraphs, around 300 words unless the task says otherwise.
